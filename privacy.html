Privacy Policy

TH3 Wallet

Privacy details for the TH3 Wallet browser extension and its use of local wallet data, public TH3 addresses, signed transactions and TH3Chain API access.

Last updated: June 14, 2026

TH3 Wallet is a browser wallet for TH3Chain. It lets users create or import a wallet, view a TH3 address, check balance and transaction history, receive TH3 with a QR code, and send signed TH3 transactions.

Data Stored Locally

TH3 Wallet stores wallet data locally in the user's browser storage. The seed phrase is encrypted with the user's password. TH3 Wallet does not send seed phrases, private keys, or passwords to TH3Chain servers.

Data Sent To TH3Chain API

To provide wallet functionality, TH3 Wallet may send public TH3 wallet addresses and signed transaction data to the TH3Chain API. This is used only to fetch balances, UTXOs, transaction history, and to broadcast signed transactions.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or personal identity information through the extension.
  • We do not collect passwords, private keys, or seed phrases.
  • We do not sell user data.
  • We do not use user data for advertising or credit scoring.

Third Parties

TH3 Wallet connects to TH3Chain infrastructure such as the public API and explorer. Public blockchain data may be visible on the TH3Chain network and explorer.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: contact@th3chain.cloud
