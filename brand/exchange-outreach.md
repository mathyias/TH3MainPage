## Important Safety Notes

Do not trust random people claiming guaranteed CoinGecko or CoinMarketCap listings.

Use only official listing forms and official exchange communication channels.

Do not send private keys, wallet.dat files, seed phrases, or server credentials to any listing provider.

Avoid paying unknown intermediaries for guaranteed listings.
