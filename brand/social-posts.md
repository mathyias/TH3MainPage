# TH3Chain Social Posts

## Launch Post

TH3Chain is live.

TH3 is an independent KAWPOW Proof-of-Work chain with public mining, a browser wallet, explorer, API, and active pool payouts.

Website: https://th3chain.cloud  
Wallet: https://wallet.th3chain.cloud  
Explorer: https://explorer.th3chain.cloud  
Pool: https://pool.th3chain.cloud  

## Mining Post

TH3 mining is open.

Algorithm: KAWPOW  
Pool: stratum+tcp://pool.th3chain.cloud:3333  
Use your TH3 wallet address as username.

Start here: https://pool.th3chain.cloud/start.html
